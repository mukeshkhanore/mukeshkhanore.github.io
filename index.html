Computational physicist · PhD researcher · Data scientist

Machine learning for large-scale atomic simulation of perovskites.

Computational physicist with 8+ years of research experience, currently completing a PhD at Charles University. I use machine learning to make large-scale physics simulations dramatically faster while keeping quantum-level accuracy work that combines model training, scientific software development, and high-performance computing on national and European supercomputers. Proficient in Python and FORTRAN, with strong foundations in numerical methods, data pipelines, and GPU workflows. Two years of industrial R & D experience in machine learning, forecasting, and data analytics, including leading small teams and working with cloud platforms (Google Cloud, TensorFlow).
